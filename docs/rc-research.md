# Research Consultancy: research brief

Source notes behind the website's content, gathered September 2026 from research-consultancy.org,
RC's Instagram (@researchconsultancyrc) and Facebook (facebook.com/researchconsultancyrc), and
M. S. Khan, "Fostering the Next Generation of Physician-Scientists in Developing Countries", ACC
Fellows in Training Section, January 2024.

## Positioning

- Research Consultancy (RC) is the parent organisation. It serves **international** medical students
  and graduates, mostly IMGs preparing for the U.S. Match (students from Pakistan, India, Ghana,
  Canada and elsewhere).
- RCOP (Research Council of Pakistan) is RC's subsidised programme for students in Pakistan and has
  its own website. **The RC website never mentions RCOP.** RC may present RCOP's statistics as its own.
- Taglines: "Creating Future Leaders in Medicine", "Publish Earlier. Match Stronger. Lead in Medicine."
- Tone of RC's own posts: warm, proud of mentees by name, concrete about numbers and journals,
  honest disclaimer on every workshop post ("RC neither conducts nor guarantees publications").

## Numbers

- 3,500+ students mentored since 2016; 600+ publications (ACC article, RC site).
- 70% women; one-third from rural areas (ACC article, borrowed).
- 20+ mentees in U.S. postdoctoral research posts (Yale, UT Southwestern, Baylor, UMMC, Emory, Houston Methodist, Oklahoma, Endeavor Health).
- Match: 90% (2024–25 batch); 80% (2025–26 batch, Match 2026).
- 2025 in review: 20 workshops, 150+ peer-reviewed publications, 100+ conference abstracts.
