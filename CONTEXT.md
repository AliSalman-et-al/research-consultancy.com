# Research Consultancy

Research Consultancy (RC) trains international medical students to do and publish clinical research, from a first course through to the residency Match.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
Anyone who takes an RC course or program. This is the default word for the reader in all public copy.
_Avoid_: Mentee (as a general term), learner, participant, client

**Mentee**:
A student in relation to one named mentor, as in "Dr. Cheema's mentee". Never used on its own to mean students in general.

**Faculty**:
The four U.S. physician-scientists who lead RC and teach its courses: Dr. M. Shahzeb Khan, Dr. Kaneez Fatima, Dr. M. Shariq Usman and Dr. Tariq Jamal Siddiqi.
_Avoid_: Instructors, teachers, founders (as a group name)

**Research analyst**:
A specialized, senior mentor, highly skilled in statistics, who helps mentors and project groups analyze their data correctly (for example in the NIS or NRD) and gives statistical guidance.
_Avoid_: Statistician, data analyst

**Mentor**:
An experienced researcher, usually a former RC student, who guides a project group to publication and reviews its work.
_Avoid_: Tutor, coach, near-peer (in public copy)

**Beginner**:
A student with no research experience, usually early in medical school. The reader the homepage serves first.
_Avoid_: Novice, newcomer

**Match applicant**:
A final-year student or graduate preparing to apply for U.S. residency through the NRMP Match.
_Avoid_: Applicant (unqualified), IMG (in copy for beginners)

### Teaching

**Course**:
A subject RC teaches, such as Level 1 or CDC WONDER. A course exists whether or not it is running.
_Avoid_: Workshop, program (except Match Mentorship), masterclass (except in the name "Meta-Analysis Masterclass")

**Class**:
One dated run of a course, with its own dates, registration period and payment link. RC schedules classes when it has capacity and announces them about a month ahead, not on a fixed calendar.
_Avoid_: Session, cohort, batch, intake

**Registration**:
How a student takes a place in a class, by paying through the class's payment link. Registration is open, opening soon (announced with a date), or closed (no class announced, or the class is full).
_Avoid_: Enrollment (as a noun), sign-up, booking

**Match Mentorship**:
RC's 12-month program for Match applicants. Students apply and are selected, so it has applications rather than registration.
_Avoid_: Match program, mentorship course
